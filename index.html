Could one silly dice roll change your life?

Maybe not overnight — but a tiny moment of fun can remind you of something big: your health, happiness, and work-life balance deserve attention. Sick or Shift? is a free online decision dice game for those Sunday-night “do I really have to work tomorrow?” moments.

Playful decisions, real-life perspective

Roll an even number and it's “go to work.” Roll an odd number and it's “call in sick.” It's pure chance, not professional or medical advice. What makes this little game special isn't the answer — it's the question it gets you asking: am I getting enough rest, and how am I really feeling?

A better balance starts with listening to yourself

Healthy work-life balance is about making room for sleep, recovery, friends, family, and the things that recharge you. If you're actually ill, base your decision on your symptoms and appropriate health guidance, not a dice roll. If you're constantly exhausted or dreading work, consider what support, time off, or changes might help.

Frequently asked questions

Should I go to work tomorrow or call in sick?

If you're unwell, consider your symptoms, workplace policy, and medical guidance as needed. The dice are for entertainment, not for deciding whether you're well enough to work.

Can a dice game improve my work-life balance?

Not by itself. But a playful pause can encourage reflection about your routines, rest, and boundaries. Meaningful improvements come from the choices you make afterward.

Is Sick or Shift free?

Yes! It's a free browser game with a 50/50 dice roll and no sign-up required.
